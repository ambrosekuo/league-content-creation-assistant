What it is

Poststream Assistant helps creators turn long gaming streams into short-form videos, then send finished drafts to platforms such as TikTok and YouTube. Processing and uploads run on your machine; this website describes the service for app review and account authorization.

  • Prepare clips locally Cut, score, caption, and mix shorts from VODs without uploading your archive to this site.
  • Publish with platform APIs Connect your TikTok or YouTube account so the desktop uploader can place drafts or private videos for you to finish in-app.
  • You stay in control Nothing is auto-published as a public post from this tool’s TikTok draft flow. You review captions, covers, and timing before anything goes live.

TikTok integration

Poststream Assistant uses TikTok’s Content Posting API with the video.upload scope to send videos into your TikTok draft inbox. Authorization uses OAuth. Tokens stay on the device that runs the uploader. See the Privacy Policy for what is stored and how to revoke access.
