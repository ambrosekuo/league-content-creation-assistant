Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Poststream Assistant (“we”, “the Service”) handles information when you use the website at this domain and the related desktop uploader that connects to third-party platforms such as TikTok and YouTube.

1. Who we are

Poststream Assistant is a creator tool for preparing and publishing gaming content. The public website explains the Service and hosts this policy and our Terms. Video processing and uploads run on software you operate on your own computer (or your own cloud jobs), not on this static website.

2. Information we collect

Depending on how you use the Service, the following may be involved:

  • Account authorization data. When you connect TikTok or YouTube through OAuth, the platform may issue access and refresh tokens, an open ID or channel ID, granted scopes, and expiry times. Those tokens are stored locally on the device running the uploader (for example under a local secrets folder), not on this website.
  • Content you choose to upload. Video files, titles, descriptions, and related metadata that you select in the uploader are sent directly to the destination platform’s APIs.
  • Website analytics. This static site does not run first-party analytics cookies. Your browser and hosting provider (for example GitHub Pages) may process standard server or CDN logs such as IP address, user agent, and requested URLs.

3. How we use information

  • To authorize the uploader against TikTok, YouTube, or other platforms you enable.
  • To upload drafts or private videos on your behalf according to the scopes you grant.
  • To operate and secure this informational website.
  • To respond if you contact us about privacy or access requests.

We do not sell personal information.

4. TikTok

Poststream Assistant may use TikTok’s developer products, including the Content Posting API with the video.upload scope, to place videos into your TikTok draft inbox after you authorize the app. We use TikTok login credentials and related API responses only to provide that upload functionality. TikTok’s own policies also apply when you use TikTok. You can revoke Poststream Assistant’s access from your TikTok account settings / authorized apps.

5. YouTube / Google

If you connect YouTube, the uploader uses Google OAuth and the YouTube Data API to create videos (typically as private until you finish them in YouTube Studio). Google’s privacy terms apply to data Google processes. You can revoke access from your Google Account permissions.

6. Sharing

Content and tokens are shared with the platforms you connect (TikTok, Google/YouTube, and any other APIs you configure) solely to perform the actions you request. We do not share your data with advertisers. Hosting for this website may involve GitHub or another static host acting as a processor for HTTP requests.

7. Retention

OAuth tokens remain on your device until you delete them, re-authorize, or they expire. Platform copies of uploaded videos are retained under each platform’s rules. This website does not maintain a user account database.

8. Security

Keep your device, .env files, and local token stores private. Use HTTPS redirect URIs registered in each developer console. We cannot guarantee absolute security of any internet-connected system.

9. Children

The Service is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Your choices

  • Revoke platform access in TikTok or Google account settings.
  • Delete local token files and stop using the uploader.
  • Contact us to ask questions about this policy.

11. Contact

Privacy questions: open an issue on the Poststream Assistant GitHub repository.

12. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the Service after updates means you accept the revised policy.
